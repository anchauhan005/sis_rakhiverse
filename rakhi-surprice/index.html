Dear My Sister & Friend,

I don't always say it, but from the day we met in 12th, you were never just a friend to me. You were my sister too, and you knew that I was your brother.

We've shared so many little moments — laughter, silly fights, annoying each other, long conversations, and countless memories. Somehow, through all of them, our friendship and sisterhood became one of the most special parts of my life.

You have also been there for me when I was upset or not feeling well. You cared for me, checked on me, and made sure I was okay. And whenever I needed someone to stand by me, you took my side.

On this Rakhi, I just want you to know that I'll always be there to support you, annoy you a little, listen to you, celebrate your happiness, and stand beside you whenever you need me.

Some people come into our lives as friends and quietly become family. You are one of those people for me. 🌸

I hope life gives you all the happiness, success and love you deserve. And I hope our friendship and sisterhood keeps creating beautiful memories.

Happy Raksha Bandhan to my sister,
